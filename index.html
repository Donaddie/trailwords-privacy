TrailWords Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

Overview

TrailWords is an offline word puzzle game. This release does not create user accounts, display advertising, use analytics SDKs, or send gameplay data to our servers.

Data stored on your device

TrailWords stores game progress, scores, coins, settings, streaks and achievements locally on your device so your progress can be restored when you reopen the app.

Data we do not collect

TrailWords does not collect personal information, advertising identifiers, location, contacts, photos, microphone data, financial information, or account credentials.

Network access

The app does not require internet access for normal gameplay and does not declare the Android INTERNET permission in this release.

Backups

Android may include app data in device or cloud backups depending on your device and Google backup settings. You can reset all TrailWords progress from the app's Settings screen.

Children

TrailWords is a general-audience word puzzle game and does not knowingly collect personal data from children or adults.

Future changes

If a future version adds analytics, advertising, online accounts or other data collection, this policy and the Google Play Data Safety declaration will be updated before release.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the developer contact address shown on TrailWords’ Google Play store listing.
